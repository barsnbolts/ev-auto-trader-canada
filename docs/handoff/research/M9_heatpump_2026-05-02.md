## Hyundai (all CONFIRMED standard)

### Ioniq 5 2025 — STANDARD on all trims
- Source: Hyundai Canada showroom IONIQ 5 page — "Heat Pump System... The standard system absorbs external heat energy and converts it to cabin heating"
- Confirms across: Preferred RWD (Standard Range), Preferred RWD Long Range, Preferred AWD Long Range, N
- Confidence: **High**

### Ioniq 5 2026 — STANDARD on all trims (no spec change announced)
- Hyundai Canada showroom carries forward the "standard heat pump" language for the 2026 model year
- Confidence: **High** (carryover)

### Ioniq 6 2025 — STANDARD on all trims
- Source: Hyundai Canada showroom IONIQ 6 — "Heat Pump System: This standard smart technology helps reduce loss of electric driving range in cold winter weather"
- Confirms across: Preferred Long Range RWD, Preferred Long Range AWD, Preferred Long Range AWD w/Ultimate Pkg
- Confidence: **High**

### Ioniq 9 2026 — STANDARD on all trims
- Source: Hyundai US 2026 IONIQ 9 spec PDF (page-level table):
  | HVAC          | S | SE | SEL | Limited | Calligraphy |
  | Heat Pump     | S | S  | S   | S       | S           |
- Driving.ca review: "All also feature a more-efficient heat pump"
- Confidence: **High**
- Note: Canadian trim names differ from US (Essential / Preferred / Calligraphy). Per
  Hyundai Canada showroom, all Canadian Ioniq 9 trims pull from the same hardware
  spec pool — heat pump is standard across.
