## Inventory data — national coverage

- `data/dealers.json` covers ON (47), BC (14), QC (11), AB (7), MB (1) = 80 dealers
- `data/units.json` is regenerated by `scripts/build_units_from_at.py` — **does not currently capture province on the unit itself** (all 100 units have `province: null`). The page filters by `dealer.province` instead, which works but means cross-province moves go undetected.
- Default region was `Greater Golden Horseshoe` until 2026-05-03 — now `All Canada`. If you change it back, 53 dealers worth of inventory disappear.
- **Cron** at `~/Library/LaunchAgents/com.evautotrader.refresh.plist` runs `scripts/refresh_daily.sh` daily 7am ET → re-scrape AutoTrader, snapshot, predeploy, push. Monitor `logs/cron.log`.
- Snapshot history: `data/snapshots/` has 4 files (2025-04-01 / 04-15 + 2026-05-01 / 05-02). Cron will append daily.

### Open question (research-tier)
Should `units.json` carry `province` directly (denormalize from dealer)? Pro: lets us page-filter without joining. Con: stale if dealer moves. Recommend: keep dealer-join filter (current state), expose a "national mix" badge in the inventory header.
