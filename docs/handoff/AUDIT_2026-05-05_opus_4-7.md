## 3 · Claude 4.7 context-collapse findings (highest-leverage)

The research agent dug into late-2025/early-2026 Anthropic guidance + the
power-user blog posts. Three findings that **contradict older norms**:

### **Compact at 60%, not 80-95%**
Older "let the warning trigger compact" advice is wrong on degradation grounds.
By the time the bar fills the model is already drifting; the summary inherits
the rot. Run `/context` to track. **You should be `/compact`-ing earlier
than your habit is letting you.**

### **`/clear` > `/compact` for unrelated tasks**
Document-First pattern: dump progress to a markdown file, then `/clear`.
Treats filesystem as durable memory, context as scratch. Your `LEARNINGS.md`
+ `milestone.py` ritual is **already aligned with this** — make sure
`milestone.py` actually runs `/clear` after the dump.

### **Prompt cache breaks on model switching**
Toggling Opus↔Sonnet (which you JUST did flipping to extra-high) **breaks
the cache** and rebuilds it. Cost spike up to 20× per Anthropic's own data.
**Pin the model per session.** If you need Opus for review, finish your
Sonnet work first, dump to file, `/clear`, then start fresh on Opus.
