## Key findings (Gemini > ChatGPT on these)

### EVAP $50,000 transaction-value cap [CRITICAL]
- $5,000 BEV rebate for vehicles with **final transaction value ≤ $50,000** (imported)
- No cap for Canadian-made EVs (E-GMP cars are Korea/US-built → all subject to the cap)
- Transaction value = MSRP + freight/PDI + accessories + dealer fees, pre-tax
- Lease proration:
  - ≥48 months → $5,000
  - 36 months → $3,750
  - 24 months → $2,500
  - 12 months → $1,250
- Effective 2026-02-16
- Source: tc.canada.ca/en/road-transportation/innovative-technologies/electric-vehicles/electric-vehicle-affordability-program

### Quebec Roulez vert step-down schedule
- 2024: $7,000
- 2025: $4,000
- 2026: $2,000
- 2027-01-01: $0 (program terminates)
- Eligibility tied to **registration date**, not order/deposit date
- Source: quebec.ca/en/transports/electric-transportation/financial-assistance-electric-vehicle/about-roulez-vert-program

### BC Go Electric — paused indefinitely
- Passenger rebate paused May 2025; not revived as of spring 2026
- Commercial rebates returning
- Source: biv.com/news/transportation/bc-scraps-provincial-ev-rebates-permanently-amid-federal-renewal-11844186

### BC PST progressive vehicle surtax
- Up to $54,999.99: 7%
- $55,000–$55,999.99: 8%
- $56,000–$56,999.99: 9%
- $57,000–$124,999.99: 10%
- $125,000–$149,999.99: 15%
- $150,000+: 20%
- Source: BC Chamber, Taxccount
